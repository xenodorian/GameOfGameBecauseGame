PART 1. WEAPONS AND CHARACTER ART (highest priority; the user asked for this)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The user asked for: every character has a weapon, animations checked closely for
fluidity, projectiles fire from the correct spot, and polish wherever possible.
Only part of this is done.

W1. Knight has no sword during slash or thrust (and hurt, spellcast, shoot).
    Where: game/tools/build_lpc.py, CHARACTERS["knight"], sword layers.
    Verified: knight.png sheet. Row 2 (slash) and row 3 (thrust) show the shield
    and body with no sword. The arming sword only has idle, walk, combat_idle,
    and hurt frames in the LPC generator (Glob of weapon/sword/arming/universal).
    The attack is the main action of the knight, so this is the biggest visible
    gap.
    Tried: longsword_alt slash exists but its frames are 128 px on a different
    grid (weapon/sword/longsword_alt/slash/longsword_alt.png, viewed). build_lpc.py
    crops 64 px cells, so it would be cropped wrong. Not tried: dagger thrust for
    the knight (not done; would look wrong with the arming sword elsewhere).
    To settle: either add an offset-aware 128 px path to build_lpc.py, or find a
    64 px sword set with slash and thrust, or draw the frames by hand. Check that
    the chosen sword keeps the same blade length across idle, walk, and attack.

W2. Ranger has no bow in idle, walk, jump, slash, thrust, or spellcast.
    Where: CHARACTERS["ranger"] bow layers in build_lpc.py.
    Verified: the normal bow in the LPC generator has only shoot and hurt
    (Glob of weapon/ranged/bow/normal/universal). A broader glob found only
    great-bow walk frames, which are a different, larger bow.
    Result in game: the bow appears only during shoot and hurt.
    To settle: decide whether to use the great bow walk frames for walk only
    (visual check needed), or to hold the bow by reusing the shoot first frame
    for idle (likely looks wrong), or accept the gap and document it in CLAUDE.md.

W3. Bone weaver has no bow in idle, walk, jump, or the melee rows.
    Where: CHARACTERS["weaver"], added in d6e2ded.
    Same cause as W2 (the same bow set). Shoot and hurt only.
    Also check: the weaver's shoot arrows are bone-coloured (V2). The bow is
    steel-coloured in the sheet, so the bow and the bone arrows may not match.
    Not verified visually at sprite scale (the screenshots are too small).

W4. Mage staff is missing on slash and shoot.
    Where: CHARACTERS["mage"], staff layers.
    Verified: staff sheets (weapon/magic/simple) have walk, thrust, spellcast,
    hurt. Slash and shoot are hidden. Idle and jump now use the walk frame (d6e2ded).
    Mage slash in the game still shows the staff hidden. Check whether this
    looks wrong when the mage swings.

W5. Rat dagger: visible on walk, slash, thrust, hurt, idle (walk pose), and jump
    (walk pose). Not on spellcast or shoot (hidden). Dagger rows are 64 px.
    Verified: rat.png sheet, rows 2 and 3 show the dagger.
    Open question: the rat's dagger in idle is the walk-pose dagger; check that
    the hand position matches the idle body pose. Not verified at scale.

W6. Grunt, archer, warlord, rat king, and mud revenant have no weapons.
    The user's request said "all characters". This log reads that as the five
    playable characters (knight, ranger, mage, rat, bone weaver). The enemies are
    listed here because the wording could include them.
    Where: CHARACTERS in build_lpc.py. The archer is a skeleton with no bow.
    The warlord has armour and helmet but no weapon. Rat king has a crown but no
    weapon.
    To settle: the user should say whether enemies need weapons.

W7. Zombie (mud_revenant) and skeleton (weaver, archer) have no jump frames.
    Where: body sheets. Verified: body/bodies/zombie has no jump folder; skeleton
    has idle, walk, slash, thrust, spellcast, shoot, hurt only.
    Result: jump uses walk (zombie) or idle (skeleton, weaver). Check the feel of
    jump for both characters.

W8. Sheet has stray frames beyond the animation's frame count.
    Where: game/tools/build_lpc.py, compose(). When one layer has more frames
    than the first layer, the frame list grows and extra columns get filled with
    frames from the longer layer only.
    Verified: rat.png, row 0 (idle), columns 2 to 8 show a dagger with no body.
    The game reads only the first 2 idle columns (ANIM_ROWS idle count 2), so
    gameplay is not affected. The sheet is still messy and could confuse a future
    reader or tool. Fix: crop each row to the animation's frame count in compose().
    Not changed in this session.

W9. Weapon layering and z-order are hard-coded.
    Where: build_lpc.py CHARACTERS zPos values. Known from CLAUDE.md: the knight
    shield bg is 3 in the code but its definition says 6 (harmless). Not verified
    against all characters.

W10. Idle and jump weapon fallback is a design change.
     Where: compose() in build_lpc.py, "weapons only reuse their walk pose on idle
     and jump". Logged in CLAUDE.md decisions. The rule "weapon layers never use
     the attack fallback" still holds for slash, thrust, spellcast, shoot, hurt.
     Needs a visual check at sprite scale on the knight (sword in idle) and mage
     (staff in idle).

W11. Art was never checked at sprite scale after d6e2ded.
     Screenshots (game/tests/shots/c_*.png) are 960 by 540. Sprites are small;
     weapon details are hard to judge. A zoomed crop or a sheet-level contact
     sheet is needed.

PART 2. ANIMATION FLUIDITY AND PROJECTILE ORIGINS (not started)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A1. Projectile origins not audited.
    Where: game/src/abilities.js, "shoot" case around line 107 to 120. Player
    arrows spawn at x = p.x + p.w (right) or p.x - 8 (left), y = p.y + 12. Hostile
    arrows (fireHostile) use the enemy's x and y + 2 from the archer's code.
    Not verified: whether the bow tip or the hand on the sprite matches these
    points on each frame of the shoot row. The shoot row has 13 frames, and the
    arm position changes across them. A spawn point that is correct on frame 0
    may be wrong on the frame where the arrow is released.
    Also: the weaver's arrow and the ranger's arrow share the same spawn code.
    To settle: draw a per-frame origin table from the sheet (hand pixel per frame),
    then pick the spawn point from the frame that is showing when the shot fires.

A2. Mage beam origin not audited.
    Where: abilities.js, beam with body p and dir p.facing. The beam starts from
    the body position, and the staff tip is drawn separately (V6 screenshot shows
    the beam leaving the staff tip; not measured in pixels).

A3. Hit and slash effect origins not audited.
    Where: faceRect and the slash/thrust hitboxes. The visible slash arc and the
    hitbox are drawn in different places in code. Not measured.

A4. Animation frame timing not reviewed frame by frame.
    Where: game/src/anim.js ACTION_LENGTH and ANIM_ROWS. Spellcast 0.3 s over 7
    frames, shoot 0.3 s over 13 frames, slash 0.2 s over 6 frames, thrust 0.24 s
    over 8 frames, jump 0.25 s over 5 frames. Walk uses walkDist / 6 px per frame.
    Not verified: any of these feel right in play. No frame was watched in motion.

A5. Idle frame count is 2 (ANIM_ROWS idle [0, 2]) and the idle loop changes every
    0.5 s (time * 2). A single breathing frame is not used. Could look stiff.
    Not verified.

A6. Landing and hurt poses.
    Landing uses idle col 0 for landT, which may read as a snap back to standing.
    Not verified.

A7. Sheet credits: CREDITS.txt was regenerated in d6e2ded (dagger and bow entries
    added). The credits file is generated, so a manual edit would be lost.
